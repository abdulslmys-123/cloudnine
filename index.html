Our Story

Beauty is our passion

Mera's Beauty Bar is a premium beauty destination in the heart of Rawdat Zayed, Sheikh Zayed — where skilled hands and genuine care come together for you.

We believe every woman deserves to feel effortlessly beautiful. Whether it's a flawless lash set, a fresh gel manicure, a silky blowout, or a skin-renewing facial — we pour care into every single appointment.

Our certified team works with quality products in a clean, welcoming space that feels like your own personal retreat.
